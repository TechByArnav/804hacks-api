What the research shows

Cited findings on AI & code emissions

Peer-reviewed studies and open tools that quantify the carbon footprint of AI training, inference, and developer workflows.

Training emissions at scale

An analysis of 5,227 Hugging Face models estimates aggregate training emissions of approximately [1]60,000 metric tons CO₂e — comparable to the annual emissions of ~42,000 cars. Annual training emissions grew from ~450 tCO₂e (2020–2021) to over 40,000 tCO₂e (2024–2025), nearly two orders of magnitude in five years [1].

Training vs. inference

Hugging Face's BLOOM model emitted ~25 metric tons of CO₂ during training; doubling to ~50 metric tons when including hardware manufacturing, infrastructure, and post-training operation [2]. GPT-3 training was estimated at over 500 metric tons of CO₂ [2].

Prompt-level emissions

A study of 14 LLMs found that reasoning-enabled models produce [3]up to 50 times more CO₂ emissions than concise response models. The most accurate reasoning model (70B parameters) produced three times more emissions than similarly sized concise-answer models [3]. Users can reduce emissions by prompting for concise answers and reserving high-capacity models for tasks that genuinely require them [3].

LLM-assisted coding

LLM-assisted code generation leads to an average [4]32.72× higher carbon footprint than manual software development (range: 19.92×–43.81×). For LLM-assisted workflows, 98.68% of the carbon footprint comes from querying the LLM itself [4].

Virginia grid context

CarbonSense measures against the PJM Dominion (VA-DOM) zone. PJM's average CO₂ emission rates fell [5]43% from 2005 to 2023 as cleaner resources replaced older units [5]. In Virginia, natural gas represents 52% of installed capacity, with hydro at 15% and nuclear at 14% [6]. Solar represents 41% of new interconnection requests in the state [6].

Measurement tools

Open-source tools like [7]CodeCarbon track CO₂ from local compute (CPU, GPU, RAM) using regional grid carbon intensity, while EcoLogits estimates GenAI API impacts across providers like OpenAI, Anthropic, and Mistral [7]. Frameworks such as LLMCarbon model end-to-end LLM carbon footprints including training, inference, experimentation, and storage [8].
